# CopperJack

Bring Claude or ChatGPT into your deals. Review photos, work through budgets, and tackle the deal together, from the first walkthrough to the final punch list. You need a CopperJack account.

## Connect your account

In Claude, open the CopperJack connector in the directory and select Connect to Claude. If you installed the optional plugin, open it under Customize > Plugins and use its Connectors tab to connect CopperJack. The connector works without the skill. If needed, enable CopperJack in the chat's tools menu.

In ChatGPT, install CopperJack from the directory and connect your account when prompted. Sign in to CopperJack and check the organization and access shown on the consent screen before you approve. Start a new chat after connecting. Access follows your existing organization permissions.

If your connection still has read-only access from an earlier release, reconnect and check that the consent screen includes edits. Organization owners and admins can manage AI edits and the monthly AI deal-creation allowance in Settings > AI. A role restriction still applies after reconnecting.

## Things to try

- Look through the property photos with me. What am I missing?
- Where can we tighten up this rehab budget?
- Turn my walkthrough notes into tasks.
- Write up an offer summary and save it to the deal's Docs.

## Requested changes

With read-and-edit access and AI edits enabled, your assistant can save requested notes, tasks, deal status changes and photo imports directly. Photo imports accept supported shared Google Drive, Dropbox and Google Photos links.

Ledger entries, underwriting assumptions, rehab items, playbooks, rehab and task templates, Ledger settings and new deals use a preview first. Review the proposed change and confirm before it is saved. Changes follow your role and the deal's locks. Activity identifies the person and AI client that made the change.

## Documents and limits

Your assistant can list saved documents and read PDF and image pages, including scans. The first read can take time while pages are prepared. Reading is limited to the first 200 pages; other file types, damaged files and password-protected files cannot be read through this connection.

Your assistant can also write a PDF for a deal, such as a rehab budget review or an offer summary, and save it to the deal's Docs. It shows you a preview first, and nothing is saved until you agree.

CopperJack does not expose deletion, archiving, account administration, live MLS searches or a new ARV workflow through public Core. A saved ARV is an existing estimate, not a new valuation. Organization templates are distinct from the settings already saved on a deal. Template administration remains subject to owner/admin permissions.
