# CopperJack for Claude

Connect Claude to your [CopperJack](https://copperjack.com) account to work with
saved property information, notes, tasks, rehab details, and photos.

## Included skills

- **Task briefing**: summarize saved tasks and help you see what to work on next.
- **ARV (beta)**: compare property evidence and comparable sales to estimate a
  retail-ready value and range, with an explanation of confidence.

Both skills are optional. You can use your own workflow or valuation method.
Available capabilities depend on your CopperJack organization and permissions.

## Account connection

The plugin connects to CopperJack's hosted MCP server. When Claude requests a
connection, sign in to CopperJack and authorize access to your organization.
Credentials are entered on CopperJack's sign-in page.

## Current scope

Claude can read supported CopperJack information and inspect saved photos.
View the photos themselves in CopperJack. Document reading and updates to saved
deals are not supported. ARV beta results vary by model and effort and can
contain factual or reasoning errors. The skill does not save or change your
property's ARV. It can explain when there is insufficient evidence for an
estimate.
