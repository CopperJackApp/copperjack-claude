# Ledger spending

Read every `list_deal_ledger` page for the selected deal before computing vendor or category totals. Each page repeats **whole-deal** totals; do not add those totals together. Aggregate unique entry IDs, preserve money-in versus money-out, and convert cents to dollars only for display.

Group money-out by the saved payee for “totals by vendor.” Incoming funding is not negative vendor spending. Do not silently merge different vendor names or treat missing category/account labels as a known classification. Retain archived labels on historical entries. Memos and attachments supply saved context, not verified invoice contents.

To assess rehab spending, read the saved rehab budget and identify which Ledger money-out entries belong to rehab from their categories and other returned facts. The whole-deal money-out total can include acquisition, financing and other costs. Do not compare that total to the rehab budget as if all spending were rehab. If the classification is unclear, show the known amounts and explain what cannot be assigned.

Distinguish recorded spending so far from a forecast of total cost. Remaining budget does not establish that the work will finish within budget. Use saved remaining scope or the user's information when discussing expected future costs; label estimates and missing evidence.

Report actuals are entered separately. If they differ from Ledger totals, present the difference without inventing a reconciliation or changing either source. A blank Report actual is not evidence of no spending.

If pagination cannot finish, do not report a complete vendor total. You may quote the returned whole-deal summary with its scope and read time, while stating that the entry breakdown is incomplete. Documents are metadata only; do not claim to have opened, paid or approved an attachment.
